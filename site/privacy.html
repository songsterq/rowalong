Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 12, 2026

The short version: RowAlong does not collect, store, share, or sell any personal data. Everything the app saves stays on your Mac.

This Privacy Policy explains how Endless Rain Studio ("we", "us", or "our") handles information in connection with the RowAlong application for macOS (the "App") and this website (the "Site"). By using the App or the Site, you agree to the practices described here.

1. Information We Collect

We do not collect any information from or about you. Specifically, we do not collect:

  • Personal information such as your name, email address, or phone number
  • Account or login details (RowAlong has no accounts)
  • Workout, fitness, or health data
  • Usage data, analytics, or crash reports
  • Device identifiers, advertising identifiers, or location data

2. Information Stored on Your Device

The App saves your workout templates, preferences, session history, and overlay window position locally on your Mac so it can remember them between sessions. This data is never transmitted to us or to anyone else, and we have no way to access it. You can delete it at any time by removing the App together with its data folder at ~/Library/Application Support/RowAlong.

3. How We Use Information

Because we do not collect any information, we do not use, analyze, or process personal data for any purpose.

4. Sharing and Disclosure

We do not share, sell, rent, or trade personal information with third parties, because we do not have any.

5. Third-Party Services

The App and the Site rely on a small number of third-party services. When your device connects to them, they may receive standard technical information, such as your IP address, as part of any ordinary web request. No workout or personal data is sent to them.

  • Google Fonts serves the typefaces used by the App and the Site. When offline, the App falls back to the system font and works the same. See Google's Privacy Policy.
  • Cloudflare hosts the Site and the App download. See Cloudflare's Privacy Policy.

These providers may keep standard request logs to deliver and secure their services. Their handling of that information is governed by their own privacy policies.

6. Cookies and Tracking Technologies

Neither the App nor the Site uses cookies, web beacons, pixels, fingerprinting, or any other tracking technology. We do not use analytics or advertising services.

7. Data Retention

We retain no personal data. Data stored locally by the App remains on your Mac until you delete it.

8. Data Security

Since we do not collect or store your data on our systems, there is no personal data held by us that could be exposed in a breach. Data saved by the App is protected by your Mac's own security.

9. Children's Privacy

The App and the Site are not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

10. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on where you live, laws such as the EU and UK General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) or the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA) may give you rights to access, correct, delete, or port your personal data, or to opt out of its sale. Because we hold no personal data about you, there is nothing for us to access, correct, delete, or sell. You remain in full control of the data the App stores on your device.

11. Changes to This Policy

If our practices change, we will update this Privacy Policy and its "Last updated" date before releasing a version of the App that behaves differently.

12. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact Endless Rain Studio at CONTACT-EMAIL.
