Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 12, 2026

RowAlong is built to run entirely on your Mac. We don't run servers for the app, we don't ask you to create an account, and we don't collect, sell, or share your personal data.

What stays on your device

The app stores the following locally, in your Mac's app storage, and never transmits it:

  • Your saved workout templates
  • Your preferences, such as overlay density
  • Your session history: dates, durations, and workouts you've completed, used for the heatmap, streak, and weekly stats

You can remove all of this by deleting the app and its data folder.

What we don't do

  • No accounts or sign-in
  • No analytics, telemetry, or crash reporting
  • No advertising or third-party trackers
  • No access to the video, audio, or windows behind the overlay. It simply floats above them

Network requests

The workout logic and tone cues work offline. The only outbound request the app makes is to Google Fonts to load the overlay's typeface; Google may receive your IP address as part of that standard request, subject to Google's privacy policy. If the font can't be loaded, the overlay falls back to a system font.

This website is hosted on Cloudflare and loads fonts from Google Fonts. Those providers may process standard request data (such as IP address) to serve the pages. The site itself sets no cookies and runs no analytics. The app download is served from Cloudflare R2.

Children

RowAlong collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update the date above. Any change that involved collecting data would be called out clearly in the app's release notes.
