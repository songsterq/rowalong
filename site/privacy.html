Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 13, 2026

RowAlong is a rowing interval coach for macOS, made by Endless Rain Studio. The short version: it runs entirely on your Mac, and we collect nothing about you.

What we collect

Nothing. RowAlong has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, no crash reporting, and no telemetry. We never receive your workouts, your usage, or any personal information.

What stays on your Mac

The app stores the following locally, in the app's own storage on your computer:

  • Workout templates you save
  • Your preferences (for example, overlay size and sound settings)
  • Your session history, which powers the heatmap and streak stats

This data is never uploaded or shared. Deleting the app's data, or uninstalling the app, removes it.

Network requests

The workout generator, timer, and tone cues all work offline. The only network request the app makes is to load the Saira typeface from Google Fonts for the overlay's display text. Like any web font request, this lets Google see your IP address and browser details under Google's own privacy practices. RowAlong sends no other data with it.

This website

This site is a static page served by Cloudflare, and the app download is hosted on Cloudflare R2. Cloudflare may process standard server logs (such as IP address and user agent) to deliver the site and the download. We don't use cookies, analytics, or tracking scripts on this site, and the site loads the same Google Fonts typefaces noted above.

Children

RowAlong doesn't collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update the date at the top of this page.
